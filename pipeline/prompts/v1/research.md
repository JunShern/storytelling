# Research prompt — v1

You are a historical researcher preparing a fact sheet for an educational bedtime
story. Research the topic below using web search. Be rigorous: cross-check anything
surprising across at least two reputable sources (encyclopedias, museums, university
pages, Britannica, World History Encyclopedia, Smithsonian, etc.).

## Topic

Place: {{place_name}} ({{time_display}}, {{era}})
Event: {{event}}

## Produce a fact sheet with exactly these sections

1. **VERIFIED FACTS** — 12–18 concrete facts with approximate dates, each marked
   `[well-established]` or `[contested/estimated]`. Include numbers where known
   (population estimates, distances, prices, sizes) with appropriate uncertainty.

2. **DAILY LIFE & SENSORY DETAILS** — sounds, smells, foods, clothing, work
   rhythms; what a child or apprentice would plausibly experience. Ground in
   scholarship and flag anything speculative.

3. **MISCONCEPTIONS TO AVOID** — common myths or exaggerations the story must
   not repeat.

4. **SOURCES** — 4–8 URLs of reputable sources you actually consulted.

Return only the fact sheet, formatted with the headers above.
